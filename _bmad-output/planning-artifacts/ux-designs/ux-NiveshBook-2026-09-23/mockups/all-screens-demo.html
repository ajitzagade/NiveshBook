Partner Shares

Project A · Manage main partners and their share percentage.

Share % must add up to exactly 100% across all main partners before this can be saved. Sub-partner shares work the same way, but only within their own partner's slice — a partner's sub-partners split that partner's share, not the whole project. Click a partner's name to see just their own details.

Add Money to Project

Project A needs ₹10,00,000 for this round.

Share 50% means if the project needs ₹10,00,000, Sandip Z's normal share is ₹5,00,000. You can enter any amount — the system calculates pending or extra automatically.

Withdraw Money from Project

₹5,00,000 is available to withdraw from Project A.

Can Take is based on each partner's share % of the amount available to withdraw. Enter any amount up to Can Take — whatever isn't taken now stays tracked separately as Keep for Later, not lost.
